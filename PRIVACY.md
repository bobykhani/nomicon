# Privacy statement

**Draft for legal review.** Maintained by the owner alone under `CONSTITUTION.md` Article 6; no vote changes this file.

## What is collected

When you submit, pay or vote, Nomicon records: your account identifier and login name on the hosting platform; the content you submit; the sign-off on your commits; your reviews, with timestamps; the public blockchain address that paid your fee and the transaction hash; and the outcomes of the process. The owner also records the Canadian-dollar value of each payment at receipt, for tax purposes.

## Why

To run the publication process, to compute vote weight, to make every decision verifiable by anyone, and to meet the owner's legal and tax obligations.

## Where it goes

Into the public repository and its public ledger, which anyone may copy. Payments are on a public blockchain. Nothing is sold or shared beyond that; nothing needs to be, because it is already public.

## How long

Indefinitely, because the ledger must remain replayable. Removal happens only where `CONSTITUTION.md` Article 5 or the law requires it, and is recorded as a redaction that keeps the hash of what was removed.

## Your choices

Do not participate if you need any of this to be private. Nothing is collected from readers. You may ask the privacy contact in `NOTICES.md` about your information, and you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
